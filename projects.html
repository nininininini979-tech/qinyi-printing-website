Case 01 / Art & cultural retail

A puzzle designed as a complete gift object.

The program combines printed puzzle pieces, a matching retail box and a presentation format that keeps the artwork central.

FormatCustom jigsaw puzzle and coordinated printed box
Factory scopePrepress, printing, mounting, die-cutting, finishing, assembly and packing
Quality focusArtwork color, piece separation, edge quality, component count and packaging match
Buyer fitArtists, museums, cultural retail and branded gifts

Case 02 / Corporate communication

Campaign artwork turned into a tactile format.

Qinyi's supplied portfolio documents corporate and destination-marketing puzzles where branded artwork, puzzle construction and the outer package operate as one visual system.

FormatFlat jigsaw puzzle with printed retail or presentation packaging
DevelopmentArtwork adaptation, size and piece-count selection, dieline review
Quality focusBrand color reference, image registration, cut consistency and pack-out
Buyer fitCorporate campaigns, destinations, events and employee gifts

Case 03 / Structural paper play

Flat printed parts engineered for assembly.

A 3D paper puzzle adds structural testing, slot fit and assembly instructions to the normal print and die-cut workflow.

FormatPrinted die-cut paper components, instructions and box
DevelopmentStructure review, fit testing, tooling and assembly sequence
Quality focusPart identification, slot tolerance, fold integrity and complete sets
Buyer fitEducation, aviation, tourism, museums and licensed themes
