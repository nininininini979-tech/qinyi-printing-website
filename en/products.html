Documented formats / Custom production

Start with a proven format or a new structure.

Qinyi manufactures jigsaw and 3D paper puzzles, board and card games, and printed gift packaging. The ranges below separate existing jigsaw tooling from project-specific options so buyers can qualify a brief before requesting a quote.

Documented range: 24 to 1,000 pieces
Documented finished-side range: 14 to 91.3 cm
Two stock round diameters shown in the portfolio
Typical starting quantity: 300 sets for regular jigsaws*

Product family 01

Jigsaw & art puzzles.

Use an existing cutting format for an efficient start, or commission a custom outline and piece system after engineering review.

24-56 pieces

Early learning & shaped sets

Documented formats include 14 x 14, 20 x 14, 21 x 29.7, 26 x 18, 30 x 14 and 35 x 25 cm. Large-piece shapes and age grading are confirmed from the intended market.

100-300 pieces

Education, gifts & promotions

Existing examples span 20 x 20 to 49 x 36 cm, with square, rectangular and shaped options. A 48-piece 91.3 x 61.3 cm floor-puzzle format is also documented.

500-1000 pieces

Art, retail & collector editions

Existing 500-piece formats span 40 x 58 to 75 x 55 cm. Documented 1000-piece formats include square, rectangular and 67.5 cm round editions.

Product family 02

3D paper puzzles.

Printed, die-cut sheets can be engineered into vehicles, architecture, animals and display scenes. A workable brief defines the assembled dimensions, component count, slot and tab logic, material direction, assembly sequence, instructions, target age and pack size. Existing artwork or a reference sample can be used as the starting point.

Product family 03

Board games & cards.

Coordinate every printed component under one BOM and approved packing sequence.

Boards

Game boards & paper components

Folding boards, printed tiles, paper tokens, score pads, dividers and instruction sheets can be specified as a coordinated set.

Decks

Game & playing cards

Packing

Complete kit assembly

Product family 04

Printed gift packaging.

The documented company scope includes colour and gift boxes, folding cartons, paper bags, tags and instruction leaflets. Structure, board or paper direction, insert, closure, print finish and carton packing are matched to the product being packed.

RFQ specification matrix

What to define before price and schedule.

FormatFinished dimensions, piece or card count, assembled size, component list and intended user.
MaterialsPaper or board direction, stiffness and thickness target, plus any restricted-substance or sustainability requirement. Final stock is confirmed by sample and availability.
Print & colourArtwork coverage, CMYK or named spot colours, colour reference, critical brand colours and proofing route.
Surface & conversionLamination or coating direction, special effects, die-cutting, scoring, folding, gluing and edge-quality expectations. Availability is confirmed from the actual construction.
Packaging & assemblyRetail pack, insert, instructions, component collation, barcode, carton marks and master-carton limits.
Quantity & timingRegular jigsaw projects typically start at 300 sets. Other products, samples and production schedules are quoted after the full specification and files are reviewed.
Market & qualityDestination country, intended age, required tests or documents, inspection plan, acceptable tolerances and defect criteria.

* The 300-set figure applies to the company's regular jigsaw range and is not a universal MOQ. Price, capacity, tooling status, sampling and lead time remain project-specific.
