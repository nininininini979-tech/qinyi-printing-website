Print & paper since 1998

Where creative development meets production.

Qinyi Printing is a paper-product manufacturer in Dongguan, China. It makes puzzles, paper games, cards and printed packaging through design, print, converting, assembly and packing processes.

Company at a glance

Built around print and paper production.

The company brochure records an approximately 5,000 m² facility, more than 80 employees and Roland and Heidelberg printing equipment.
