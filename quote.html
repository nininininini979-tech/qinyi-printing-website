What happens next

A specification review, not an instant generic price.

Why is MOQ not shown as one number?

Product format, dimensions, material, finish, packaging and production setup change the viable quantity. MOQ is confirmed from the actual specification.

Can the team review an early-stage idea?

Yes. Begin with the intended use, audience, approximate size, quantity and any reference images. The scope still needs to be confirmed after review.

Which market information matters?

Destination country, intended user and any buyer-specified testing or documentation requirements should be supplied before production is agreed.
