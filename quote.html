Two-step RFQ

Start with the essentials.

Send a short project outline first. Qinyi will review manufacturing fit and then request the drawings, artwork and detailed specifications needed for pricing.

Step 1 of 2

A concise first review.

  • Product category and estimated quantity
  • Destination country and target date
  • Company contact details
  • Optional public reference link

This form prepares an email in your own email application. It does not upload your information or files to this website.

Only share a link that is safe to view publicly. Do not include confidential artwork, credentials or personal data.

Step 2 of 2

Detailed specification after initial review.

If the project is a potential fit, Ms. Xiao or a Qinyi team member will reply and ask for the information needed to develop a quotation.

Product specificationFinished dimensions, piece or card count, materials, construction and packaging.
Commercial scopeQuantity tiers, destination, target date and any buyer-specific requirements.
Production filesArtwork, dielines or reference files shared through a mutually agreed email or file-transfer method.
Review resultFeasibility, missing information, sampling, MOQ, price and timing are confirmed from the reviewed specification.

Do not send confidential production files until the recipient and transfer method have been confirmed. See file security guidance.
