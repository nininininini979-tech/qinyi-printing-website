Buyer questions

Know what is fixed and what varies.

Qinyi manufactures custom puzzles, paper games, cards and packaging in Dongguan. Materials, quantities, samples, timing, testing and documentation vary by project and are confirmed after review.

What products does Qinyi manufacture?

Custom jigsaw puzzles, children's educational puzzles, 1000-piece and art puzzles, 3D paper puzzles, board games, game cards, playing cards, color boxes, gift boxes, paper bags, tags and instruction leaflets.

Can Qinyi support a project from concept to packing?

Its stated process scope includes design and development, prepress, printing, tooling, die-cutting, surface finishing, assembly and packaging. The exact scope is confirmed after review.

Where is the factory?

Qinyi Printing is located in Dongguan, Guangdong, China.

What printing equipment is used?

The company brochure identifies Roland and Heidelberg printing equipment. Specific machine models and configurations should be confirmed for a project.

What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ is confirmed by product type, dimensions, material, finish and packaging specification.

Can I order a sample?

Sample type, availability, cost and timing are confirmed after the product brief and files are reviewed.

How long does production take?

Lead time is confirmed after artwork, structure, quantity, materials, finishing and packaging requirements are reviewed.

Which files should I send?

Upload available artwork, drawings, dielines, references or specification sheets. Accepted production formats are confirmed during review.

Which certificates or test reports are available?

State the destination market and required standard so the team can confirm which current documents apply. No certification should be assumed from an image alone.

What information is needed for a quote?

Provide product type, dimensions, quantity, materials, piece or card count, finish, packaging, target market, destination country, required date and available files.
