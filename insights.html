Three checklists for a faster manufacturing review.

Prepared by the Qinyi project team from the information used in first-stage puzzle, game and packaging enquiries.

Puzzles

Custom jigsaw puzzle brief

State the finished size, piece count, quantity, target audience, destination market and packing preference. Include artwork dimensions and identify any fixed color reference.

Check puzzle formats
Games & cards

Component and version checklist

List every board, card deck, token, rule sheet, insert and box. Give each file a version name and separate card faces, common backs and language editions.

Check game scope
Packaging

Printed box RFQ checklist

Provide the product dimensions, contents, paper or board preference, structure reference, printing, surface finish, insert, quantity and destination.

Separate the commercial brief from confidential production files.
